# Service Policies (sample)

## Fee reversal
Late payment fee and annual fee reversals can be granted once in 12 months for customers with good repayment history. Reversal reflects within 2 billing cycles. A reference number must be shared.

## Transaction disputes
Disputed card transactions must be raised within 30 days of the statement date. The card should be blocked if fraud is suspected. Provisional credit may be given within 10 working days. Customer receives a dispute reference number.

## Card block and replacement
Lost or stolen cards are blocked immediately on request after identity verification. Replacement card is dispatched within 7 working days to the registered address; a replacement fee may apply.

## Limit increase
Credit limit increases are offered based on spend and repayment behaviour. Customer consent is mandatory before a limit increase is applied.

## Complaint handling
Every complaint must be logged with a reference number. Standard resolution timeline is 7 working days; escalation to the grievance officer is available if unresolved after 30 days.

## Vulnerable customers
Signs of financial hardship, bereavement, illness, confusion or advanced age require a slower pace, clear explanations, and offering a callback or branch assistance. Do not pursue sales with customers showing vulnerability.
