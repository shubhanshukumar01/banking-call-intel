# Sales Playbook (sample)

## Objection: fees too high
Acceptable responses: explain fee waiver on annual spend threshold, compare rewards value against fee, offer a lower-fee card (Cashback Credit Card) if the customer's spend is low. Never promise a waiver that is not in the current offer.

## Objection: interest rate too high
Explain the rate is risk-based, mention balance transfer and EMI conversion options, never quote a rate lower than the published "onwards" rate without approval.

## Objection: already has a product
Acknowledge the existing product, identify an unmet need (e.g. travel, cashback, emergency funds) before pitching. Do not disparage competitor banks.

## Objection: needs time to think
Offer to send product details by SMS/email on the registered contact, schedule a follow-up call with consent. Do not create urgency with false deadlines.

## Objection: trust or security concern
Reassure using official channels (app, branch), remind that the bank never asks for OTP/PIN/CVV, offer to process via net banking.

## Buying signals
Questions about eligibility, documents, delivery time, limits or "how do I apply" indicate interest. Confirm consent before initiating any application.

## Cross-sell guidance
Cross-sell only after the customer's original request is resolved. Suitability first: loans and investments require a needs assessment. Insurance and investments are third-party products and must be described as such.

## Call closure
Summarise what was agreed, give reference numbers, state timelines, and ask if anything else is needed.
